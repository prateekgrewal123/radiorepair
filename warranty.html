REFURBISHED WARRANTY

Refurbished Product Warranty

Warranty coverage is product-specific and should be reviewed before purchase.

What Refurbished Means

Refurbished equipment may have been previously owned, opened, returned, inspected, cleaned, tested or repaired. The exact history and condition can vary by unit and should be described on the product listing.

Manufacturer Certification

Unless a listing expressly states otherwise, refurbished equipment sold by an independent seller should not be assumed to be manufacturer-certified or manufacturer-refurbished.

Included Components

Only components specifically listed for the actual product are included. Accessories, harnesses, adapters, mounting hardware, antennas and other items may vary.

Testing

Only functions actually inspected or tested should be represented as tested. Testing does not guarantee compatibility with every vehicle or installation configuration.

Warranty Coverage

The applicable warranty period and coverage for a refurbished item should be stated on the individual product listing or order documentation. Warranty coverage applies only to the extent expressly stated there.

Common Exclusions

Subject to applicable law and the actual warranty terms, coverage may exclude damage caused by improper installation, incorrect wiring or voltage, water or moisture, physical damage, misuse, unauthorized modification or repair, or other causes outside normal use.

Professional Installation

Professional installation is recommended when the customer is unsure about wiring, fitment or compatibility. Installation-related damage can affect warranty eligibility where permitted by the applicable terms.

Warranty Claims

Contact us with the order number, product/model, serial number where applicable, description of the problem and supporting photos or video when useful. We will explain the next step under the applicable warranty.
