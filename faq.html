FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about repair requests, refurbished equipment and orders.

Do you offer on-site repair?

On-site service can be requested where technician coverage is available. Submit your ZIP code and equipment details for review.

Is every service request confirmed immediately?

No. A request is reviewed first. Availability, scope and applicable charges are confirmed before work begins.

When do I pay for repair work?

Payment is collected after the agreed repair work is completed within the confirmed scope. Additional work requires customer approval.

What does refurbished mean?

Refurbished equipment may have prior ownership or use and may have been inspected, cleaned, tested or repaired. Check the actual listing for condition and included components.

Do refurbished products have a warranty?

Warranty coverage is product-specific. Review the individual listing and Refurbished Product Warranty page before purchase.

How long do I have to return a product?

Eligible products may be returned within 30 days after delivery, subject to the published return conditions.

Do you offer free shipping?

Complimentary shipping is offered on qualifying orders over $150 across the continental US, subject to the shipping policy.

How do product orders get paid?

Product checkout is designed for Cash on Delivery, subject to delivery and order verification.
